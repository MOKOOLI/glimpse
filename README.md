# 🧬 Glimpse
### Early detection of rare metabolic diseases from LC-HR-MS spectra — explainable, reproducible, deployable.

> "Rare diseases are not rare. They are just rarely diagnosed."

## 🎯 The Problem
Rare metabolic disorders often take months to years to diagnose. Mass spectrometry carries the signal — but it's noisy, high-dimensional, and hard to interpret.

## 💡 The Solution
Glimpse turns raw spectra into a risk score **plus** a human-readable explanation of *which* features drove that decision.
